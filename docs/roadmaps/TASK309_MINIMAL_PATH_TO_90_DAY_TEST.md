# Task309 — Minimal Path to a Valid 90-Day Test

## 1. Purpose and fixed boundaries

This roadmap is derived from the [Task309 implementation/evidence audit](../audits/TASK309_90_DAY_LAUNCH_READINESS_AUDIT.md) at `b1ce2023f60fe4691023236e3860c47a3c094bfe`. Its evidence register E00–E23 is the source for every package below. It proposes **six** packages; none is authorized for execution by publishing this document.

The aim is private, risk-aware personal bond research with **50,000 RUB** actual capital. Preserve the frozen seven-capital treatments, investment/risk policies and Task304 fractional duration-matched OFZ reference. Do not add 25m to the grid. No broad rewrite, commercial platform, broker orders or blanket historical acquisition is proposed.

The operational critical path is **WP1 + WP2 → WP3 → independent launch review → separately authorized activation**. WP4 improves fair comparative interpretation; WP5 historical research and WP6 isolated CFA observations are parallel research tracks. Full multi-year PIT reconstruction is not a frozen forward-Day0 prerequisite. A bond-only forward observation must explicitly disclose if optional research/comparisons are not yet available.

## 2. Six bounded work packages

### WP1 / recommended Task310 — Current Forward Experiment Readiness REPORT Foundation

**WHAT_ALREADY_EXISTS:** exact universe/admission/preflight, M3 audit/Unified Candidate, scoring/risk/strategy, terms/lot planner and experiment genesis validators. Existing services already produce typed blockers (audit E05–E17).

**WHAT_IS_MISSING:** one bound read-only report joining contemporary investability facts with explicit canonical IDs, joint CORE/peer/issuer coverage, 50k execution and seven-case/benchmark feasibility. Production status is UNKNOWN; this is not a claim that rows are absent.

**WHY_NEEDED:** P0-01. Passing M3/risk and verified lot/board do not establish fresh buy availability or non-qualified access; independent coverage counts can conceal an empty intersection.

**SMALLEST_SAFE_CHANGE:** repository-only development of a deterministic REPORT contract/orchestrator that validates supplied frozen universe/admission context and delegates to existing modern read-only services. Report identities, timestamps, all per-stage exclusions and final zero-lot/post-rounding risk outcomes. Reuse the seven-case/common-evidence policy. Do not call source clients, imports, terms APPLY or activation. Report deployments/operational inputs unknown when not supplied; do not create a new source acquisition route.

**ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA:** hermetic SELECT-only/pending-state tests; exact input lineage; no cohort/policy overrides; no computed return or activation. A later separately authorized operator read-only run must establish a nonempty 50k READY plan without flags, seven preserved case results, required benchmark common-date/representative evidence and exact reviewed hashes before launch. Failures generate a quantified repair proposal, not mutation.

**DEPENDENCIES:** independent Task309 review and delivery CI first. Production/VDS/source access remains a separate authorization after implementation review; existing rows preferred to unnecessary import.

**RISK:** moderate — accidental invocation of source/mutating dependencies or promotion of outdated evidence. Freshness/access boundaries must be explicit.

**CAN_RUN_IN_PARALLEL:** development can overlap WP2 documentation/source qualification and WP4/WP6 design. This is the **only recommended next task**, not an approval to start those tracks.

### WP2 — Bounded Source-Backed Scheduled Cashflow Qualification

**WHAT_ALREADY_EXISTS:** persisted MOEX event contracts; strict B2/R1/R2 replay; R3 row integrity, source-only maturity policy and canonical-observation selection; R4 field authority matrix; Task303/304 accounting consumers (audit E15, E16, E21, E22).

**WHAT_IS_MISSING:** applicable monetary amount denominator/currency, event/nominal timing, completeness and revision authority for the holdings actually used by strategy and benchmark. Actual payments and historical publication remain separate unknowns.

**WHY_NEEDED:** P0-02. A raw `value=1000`, RUB label, checksum or maturity marker cannot by itself justify per-bond cash credit. Source-row PASS is not economic-authority PASS.

**SMALLEST_SAFE_CHANGE:** start with offline, bounded field/event qualification using applicable primary issue documents or attributable source specifications. Define exact source/version/query scope and alternative official evidence if Bondization documentation remains insufficient. No universal source repair is assumed. If later code is needed, add a separately reviewed qualified boundary for consumers; do not modify R2/R3 authority flags or inject assumed amounts.

**ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA:** explicit per-bond currency/unit and event entitlement proof; unknown coupons remain null/blocking when required; no facevalue/percentage reconstruction, no schedule-profile summation, no duplicate maturity/redemption. Demonstrate source revision/drift behavior and quantity × qualified event accounting in isolated fixtures. Distinguish scheduled entitlement from actual payment confirmation. Proof may be bounded to the actual RUB holdings; FX/value_rub conversion stays excluded without proof.

**DEPENDENCIES:** current selected holdings from reviewed WP1 evidence for final scope. Qualification design/research can precede it. Any new external access, import or production inspection needs its own approval.

**RISK:** high economic-attribution risk. Do not claim that the proposed research will necessarily find sufficient documents.

**CAN_RUN_IN_PARALLEL:** yes, with WP1 development and WP3 design; production launch waits for actual qualified scope. Full historical publication proof is required only for PIT claims, not for a disclosed contemporary scheduled-flow simulation.

### WP3 — Grouped Daily Evidence, Recovery and Operator Lifecycle

**WHAT_ALREADY_EXISTS:** atomic grouped genesis, per-run daily PLAN/APPLY with state hashes/locks/audits, benchmark daily calculation, accepted-history comparison and pure seven-case aggregation (audit E14–E17, E23).

**WHAT_IS_MISSING:** durable group/date evidence and acceptance for all seven runs plus benchmark/comparison, explicit interruption/ambiguous-commit reconciliation, daily authorization model, lifecycle/monitoring and ordered missed-day recovery. Status enums are not operator procedures.

**WHY_NEEDED:** P0-03. A synchronized genesis does not guarantee synchronized 90-day observation; seven independent successful commits do not prove group/day acceptance.

**SMALLEST_SAFE_CHANGE:** reuse Task303/304 accounting. Design one deterministic group/day boundary that freezes inputs/results, preserves all seven statuses and declares acceptance only after required child/audit evidence reconciles. Explicitly specify bounded atomicity versus recoverable child progress before implementation; do not promise atomicity by nesting service commits. Add minimal operator reporting and reviewed pause/abort/horizon handling. A scheduler may invoke only the separately approved operation; it must not bypass daily authorization.

Task305's existing `audit_group` validates genesis row counts, including exactly one child snapshot. Do not reuse it unchanged as a daily group-history audit once days have been appended; reuse Task303 accounting checks inside a separately reviewed daily reconciliation boundary instead.

**ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA:** synthetic failure after any child leaves no false COMPLETE group/day; replay does not duplicate rows; unknown commit requires durable-state reconciliation before retry; benchmark/comparison fingerprints preserved; accepted dates contiguous; weekends use existing <=7 freshness; catch-up runs oldest missing calendar date first without future evidence. No stale-mark substitution or silently skipped capital case. Operator alarms identify missing inputs, drift, unaccepted day and horizon state. Define safe stop/resume and review them independently before official Day0.

**DEPENDENCIES:** WP1 bound identities/evidence and WP2 qualified flows for operational acceptance. Any schema/deploy/production daily APPLY or scheduler activation is a separately authorized follow-up, never included implicitly in this roadmap.

**RISK:** high transaction/lifecycle risk. Exact architecture and transaction contract require their own Plan Mode review; Task309 does not select an unreviewed persistence model.

**CAN_RUN_IN_PARALLEL:** hermetic development/design with WP2; integrated operational acceptance after both. Manual supervised daily operation can be considered if it demonstrably meets the same receipt/recovery gates; automatic scheduling itself is not a substitute for them.

### WP4 — Fair Passive Benchmarks and Executable Comparison Specification

**WHAT_ALREADY_EXISTS:** frozen Task304 gross fractional duration-matched OFZ reference; modern lot/risk/accounting primitives; exact personal-capital context (audit E10–E16).

**WHAT_IS_MISSING:** separately frozen simple passive OFZ and corporate buy-and-hold policies; executable lot/cash comparator; explicit cost/tax/credit-risk comparability conventions.

**WHY_NEEDED:** P1-02/03. Gross fractional excess cannot be represented as achievable after-cost 50k superiority. Higher yield is not greater risk-aware performance.

**SMALLEST_SAFE_CHANGE:** specification first. Predeclare eligible decision-date universe, transparent liquidity/issuer/duration criteria, fixed entry/horizon/exit, default/recovery/offer handling and lot/cash rules. Review fairness against BondRadar opportunity set before observing returns. Add separately named comparator outputs later; leave Task304 reference unchanged.

**ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA:** no ex-post winner selection, disappeared/defaulted instruments retained, common dates/pricing/NKD/cash accounting, gross/net labels, disclosed commissions/spread/slippage/tax assumptions and comparable exposure. After-cost/risk-adjusted claims remain unavailable unless their inputs/path/statistic are qualified. Endpoint-only evidence cannot produce maximum drawdown. No arbitrary weakening of benchmark risk constraints.

**DEPENDENCIES:** WP1/2 for implementation economics; research policy can be reviewed earlier. Actual broker/tax facts supplied by operator, not inferred.

**RISK:** selection bias and false comparability. This is a P1 improvement, not a reason to alter the frozen OFZ success rule or block a clearly labeled gross forward reference automatically.

**CAN_RUN_IN_PARALLEL:** yes. Corporate superiority claims wait for this package; frozen forward OFZ observation has its independent P0 gates.

### WP5 — Historical Validation Feasibility and Separately Labeled Replay Design

**WHAT_ALREADY_EXISTS:** A4 raw discovery, Task307 verifier, A/A1/A2 readiness/endpoints, fixed monthly schedule, source-native identity/cashflow forensic tools (audit E18–E22).

**WHAT_IS_MISSING:** qualified dated decision universe, historical terms/issuer/publication/version authority, complete economics, holdout protocol and modern walk-forward execution boundary. Reported six-year range alone does not close these gaps.

**WHY_NEEDED:** P1-01; improve pre-test confidence without inventing historical alpha or making an unbounded history project the forward critical path.

**SMALLEST_SAFE_CHANGE:** review actual frozen artifacts offline and quantify a bounded candidate interval/cohort. Decide explicitly between NON_PIT_DIAGNOSTIC replay and a PIT-correct holdout design. Reuse modern investment/strategy logic, with a separately reviewed dated-input boundary. Freeze development/holdout/stress/rolling 90/180/365 protocols before outcome inspection. Only a quantified unresolved evidence gap can justify a separate acquisition proposal.

**ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA:** unknown publications excluded from PIT; current identities/terms never historical proof; quote/publication cutoff 00:00 UTC T; monthly dates never shifted for model readiness; missing defaults/revisions/cashflows explicit. Diagnostic output cannot claim verified alpha. A failed feasibility assessment is a valid outcome; no return is required to pass a feasibility audit.

**DEPENDENCIES:** reviewed artifact access and economic scope; replay or network acquisition requires separate explicit tasks. Independent future periods/holdout address P2-01; advanced risk research P2-02 is outside the minimal packages.

**RISK:** high look-ahead/survivorship/data-snooping risk.

**CAN_RUN_IN_PARALLEL:** yes; **not a mandatory forward-Day0 dependency** unless a later reviewed experiment policy explicitly makes a pre-test claim mandatory.

**WHAT_ALREADY_EXISTS:** Task295 empty-request Dfas reader/DTO. No full CFA research or allocation workflow found (audit E05).

**ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA:** no qualified-status assumption, no claimed transferability/liquidity without evidence, no orders, no synthetic payout, no CFA portfolio-ready flag. **DEPENDENCIES:** review of research contract; concrete source evidence when supplied. No monetary allocation is a prerequisite for this observation track.

**RISK:** product/access and comparability errors.

**CAN_RUN_IN_PARALLEL:** yes, independent of bond launch critical path.

## 4. Final Day0 review checklist

1. Pin reviewed code SHA and successful exact-commit CI. Independently verify deployed schema/compatibility only under separate operator authority; code/migration presence is insufficient.
2. Bind current source identities, access/buy/qualification/tests, activity, board and canonical Bond IDs to the same dated experiment universe. No name/ticker fallback, hidden imports or assumed qualified status.
3. Verify joint CORE and source-native peer readiness, verified LegalIssuer linkage, positive nominal/RUB, frequency and required structural evidence, market/curve/liquidity freshness. Do not relax fixed gates to obtain more positions.
4. Verify actual 50k strategy and all seven frozen cases; each selected position has verified terms, positive lots, no quality flags and final risk PASS. Preserve residual cash; no redistribution or seven-trial claim.
5. Review Task304 exact modified-duration match, all representative evidence, common genesis market date and strict Decimal invariants. Keep fractional gross reference labels and comparison limitations visible.
6. Qualify source-backed scheduled cashflows actually used for strategy and benchmark, including completeness, revisions, missing amounts, principal/nominal timing and currency. No assumption injection or actual-payment claim from schedule alone.
7. Demonstrate group/day evidence acceptance, deterministic benchmark/comparison persistence or reviewed immutable receipt boundary, authorized daily operations, safe ambiguous-outcome reconciliation, contiguous calendar-day catch-up, freshness, lifecycle and monitoring ownership.
8. Freeze scope labels: gross versus net, costs/tax unknowns, no statistical-alpha claim, corporate/passive comparator availability, and isolated CFA research status. No hidden policy changes.
9. Independent review must close P0-01/02/03 with actual evidence, not this checklist or a green unit test. Obtain separate explicit activation authority. Until then **OFFICIAL_DAY0_READY=NO** and **OFFICIAL_DAY0_STARTED=false**.

Historical PIT, costs, CFA and comparator research findings must remain visible, but do not automatically turn every research limitation into a frozen forward-test P0. A claim requiring them remains blocked even if a narrower forward observation is authorized.
